# Product - Mai Quốc Triệu Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Target Users
Students, martial artists, FPT University colleagues, and sports professionals.

## Purpose
A modern, sleek portfolio website highlighting Mai Quốc Triệu's qualifications as a Vovinam Red Belt Master (Hồng đai đệ nhất cấp) and Lecturer at FPT University Cần Thơ.

## Brand Personality
- Sophisticated & Confident
- Academic & Prestigious
- Clean & Powerful

## Anti-References
- Standard SaaS-cream beige templates.
- Over-animated elements distracting from education history.
- Cheap templates with low contrast.
